Process

How an emergency job runs, start to finish.

From the first call to the insurance paperwork, the same crew stays on it.

Call dispatch
  1. 01

    Leander and the Austin metro

    Dispatch

    Call any hour. For active leaks, flooding or storm damage we aim to be on-site within an hour and make the house safe first.

  2. 02

    Stop the damage from spreading

    Containment

    Water extraction, commercial dehumidifiers and temporary board-up or roof tarps to secure the building envelope.

  3. 03

    Structural evaluation

    Inspection and plan

    We map moisture inside the drywall, check foundation level and give you a clear written scope before any rebuild starts.

  4. 04

    Framing, drywall, roofing, plumbing, tile

    Rebuild

    Our own crew rebuilds framing, hangs and finishes drywall and trim, replaces roofing, runs plumbing and completes the remodel.

  5. 05

    Final sign-off

    Handover and insurance

    A final quality check with you, then we coordinate documentation and billing directly with your homeowners' insurance.
